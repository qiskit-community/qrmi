Code of Conduct
===============

.. rst-class:: lead

   The standards of behaviour expected of all members of the QRMI community.

--------------


QRMI adopts the `Qiskit Code of Conduct`_ and expects all contributors,
maintainers, users, and community members to adhere to its principles.

We are committed to fostering an open, welcoming, inclusive, and
respectful environment for everyone participating in the project.
