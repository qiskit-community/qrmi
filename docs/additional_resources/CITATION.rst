.. _citation:

Citing QRMI
===========

.. rst-class:: lead

   Guidance on citing QRMI in academic publications, reports, and other research outputs.

--------------


.. _qrmi_overview:

QRMI Overview Paper
-------------------

QRMI is the work of `many contributors`_ from across the quantum and HPC communities.

If you use QRMI in your research, please cite the overview paper,
`Quantum Resources in Resource Management Systems`_. Citations help
support the continued development, visibility, and adoption of the project.

BibTeX citation information is available in the `QRMI citation file`_.

.. note::

   As the project evolves, updated versions of the overview paper may be
   released. When possible, cite the most recent version relevant to your work.


.. _qrmi_integrations:

Quantum-HPC Integration Paper
-----------------------------

For work focused on integration between quantum resources and HPC systems,
you may also wish to cite
`Examining QRMI as a Unified Interface for Quantum-HPC Integration`_.

BibTeX citation information is available in the `QRMI citation file`_.
